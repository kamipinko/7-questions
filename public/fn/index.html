Get on the FN Line

One paste sets up your FN folder. After that: drag files in, work comes back, tools install themselves.

1 Be on the network (once, ever)

Install Tailscale on your computer and sign in with the team account. Gabe approves your device.

Already see "Connected" in the Tailscale menu? Skip to step 2.

2 Pick who you are + your computer

Open Terminal, hit Copy, paste the whole block, press Enter, answer any prompt.





Larry: your account exists but your mailboxes may not yet — your drops land in the team slot until Gabe finishes your lane. Everything still works.

If something fights you

You're not on the network yet — do step 1, make sure Tailscale says Connected, and ask Gabe to approve your device.


"parse error" or weird broken lines


The paste got mangled. Always use the Copy button on this page — don't copy from a chat or text message.

Give it a minute, check Tailscale is Connected. If it stays, tell Thuku.
